Before you begin

  • Use HTTPS or localhost for browser media access.
  • Use headphones to reduce audio feedback.
  • Get participant consent before recording or sharing meeting content.
  • Do not share sensitive meeting links publicly.

Deployment needs

To enable real calls, connect an authenticated WebSocket signalling service, implement WebRTC offer/answer and ICE exchange, configure STUN/TURN servers and enforce room membership.
