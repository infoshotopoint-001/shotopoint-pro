Before the session

Plan for a smooth production

  • Define the topic, audience and desired outcome.
  • Confirm the guest list, consent and session duration.
  • Choose the meeting platform or public broadcast destination.
  • Check cameras, microphones, lighting, bandwidth and backup plans.
  • Confirm whether recording, editing, captions or social clips are required.

No booking is confirmed until SPP reviews the request and confirms availability.
