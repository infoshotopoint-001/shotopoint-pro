Your voice · Responsible reporting

Submit a News Story

Share a verified news tip, local report, entertainment story, sports update, technology development or an original SPP community story. Every submission is reviewed before publication.

Send your story to SPP

Required fields are marked in the form. Please do not submit rumours, private personal information or content you do not have rights to share.

Submitting does not guarantee publication. SPP editors may request verification, edit for clarity, or decline a submission. Your story remains pending until an authorised editor approves it.

Before you submit

  • Confirm names, dates, locations and important claims.
  • Provide links to original sources where possible.
  • Do not copy full articles from other publishers.
  • Do not upload or disclose private or confidential information.
  • Use the SPP category that best fits your story.
What happens next?
Your submission enters the SPP Newsroom moderation queue. An editor will review it in the Admin Control Centre before it appears publicly.
