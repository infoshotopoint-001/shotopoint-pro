Chief Operating Officer

Chief Operating Officer

At Shoto Point Productions, the Chief Operating Officer turns strategic direction into coordinated execution. The role connects teams, processes, partners and delivery standards so that the company's projects can move from ideas to reliable results.

Operational Leadership

The COO oversees the systems and day-to-day coordination that support the company's wider objectives. This includes improving workflows, monitoring delivery, supporting teams and ensuring that important operational decisions are handled with clarity and accountability.

Partnership & Client Coordination

The COO works closely with partners and clients to understand objectives, coordinate resources and maintain productive communication. The focus is on practical solutions, clear expectations and professional follow-through from the beginning of a project to completion.

THE COO BIOGRAPHY

COMRADE EMMANUEL ANOINTED AZAGBA
Co-Founder & Chief Operating Officer (COO)
Comrade Emmanuel Anointed Azagba, also known as Emmanuel Ndidi Azagba, is a Nigerian technology entrepreneur, innovator, software architect, and digital systems developer with a strong interest in technology, artificial intelligence, and digital transformation. He is the Founder and Chief Architect of Glowstar Innovative Tech Solutions (GITS Africa), a technology ecosystem focused on developing digital infrastructure and AI-driven solutions from Nigeria for African and global applications. His journey into technology began at an early age after receiving his first desktop computer at the age of 15. He later pursued his higher education at the University of Benin (UNIBEN), Edo State, where he further developed his knowledge and experience in technology and software development. Through GITS Africa, Azagba has led the development of technology initiatives and digital products designed to address practical challenges.
These include VERITAS, a real-time election intelligence and evidence-reporting platform, and JasonPay AI, a digital banking technology concept designed to improve access to financial services through artificial intelligence. Beyond technology development, Azagba is passionate about mentorship and knowledge sharing. He engages with young African technology innovators through masterclasses, academic engagements, and mentorship programmes, encouraging critical thinking, innovation, software development, and the effective use of emerging technologies.
As Co-Founder and Chief Operating Officer (COO) of SHOTO POINT Productions, Comrade Emmanuel Azagba plays a strategic role in the company's operations, growth, innovation, technology development, and long-term direction. He works closely with the Founder and CEO to support the implementation of the company's vision and coordinate key areas of its development. He brings his experience in software architecture, artificial intelligence, entrepreneurship, digital systems, and business development to SHOTO POINT's vision of building a modern African media and production company with a global outlook. His contribution strengthens SHOTO POINT's commitment to bringing together media, creativity, technology, innovation, and professional production to create new possibilities in digital storytelling and entertainment.

Team Development

The COO supports a culture of collaboration, learning and responsibility. By helping teams understand priorities and improving communication between departments, the organisation can respond faster while maintaining a consistent standard of work.
