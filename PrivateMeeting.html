Meeting platforms

Three ways to meet

External platforms open separately for reliability and account security. The SPP Meeting Room below is a front-end prototype until the live signalling and media servers are connected.

G

Google Meet

Create a meeting in Google Meet or paste an existing Meet link below to open it. Automated meeting creation from SPP requires Google Workspace OAuth/API configuration.

Google Meet is opened in a new tab, not embedded in this page.

TV

TeamViewer Meeting

Use TeamViewer Meeting for audio/video discussion and screen sharing. Paste a meeting link supplied by the organiser or open the official TeamViewer meeting information page.

This page does not create TeamViewer sessions automatically.

SP

SPP Meeting Room

Use the Shoto Point Production branded room interface for testing camera and microphone access, preparing a room ID and sharing an invitation link.
