Chief Executive Officer

At Shoto Point Productions, the Chief Executive Officer provides the strategic vision that connects creativity, people, partnerships and sustainable business growth. The role is centred on building a company that creates meaningful media while maintaining professional standards, strong relationships and a clear long-term direction.

Leadership & Vision

COMRADE SUNDAY ARIMOKWU is responsible for defining the organisation's strategic direction, establishing priorities and creating an environment where creative and operational teams can perform at their best. This includes identifying opportunities, evaluating risks and ensuring that major decisions remain aligned with the company's mission and values.

The CEO BIO

COMRADE SUNDAY ARIMOKWU
Founder and Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
SHOTOPOINT PRODUCTIONS https://www.shotopoint.com
Comrade Sunday Arimokwu is a Nigerian public servant, social worker, humanitarian, administrator, filmmaker, journalist, media entrepreneur and creative professional. His experience spans public service, leadership, community development, activism and the media industry. He is from Ekuku Agbor, Ika South Local Government Area of Delta State, and was born and raised in Uvbe Town, Edo State. He belongs to the Godspower Arimokwu Osayi and Felicia Okwudiri family of Oroma Quarters, Ekuku Agbor. His birthday is celebrated annually on 23 August.

EDUCATION AND PROFESSIONAL QUALIFICATIONS
Comrade Sunday began his education at Uvbe Primary School, Uvbe Town, where he obtained his First School Leaving Certificate in 2003. He attended Brain Touch Academy, Uvbe Town, and completed his secondary education in 2009, obtaining his National Examination Council (NECO) certificate. He obtained a Bachelor of Science degree in Political Science Education from Delta State University, Abraka, in 2013. He later attended Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria, where he earned a Master’s degree in Peace and Conflict Management in 2018 and a Master’s degree in International Affairs and Diplomacy in 2020. His professional qualifications include an Advanced Certificate Course in Journalism from the International Institute of Journalism, Jahi, Abuja, affiliated with the University of Maiduguri, completed in 2022. In 2021, he obtained the National Examination Board in Occupational Safety and Health (NEBOSH) qualification through Novella Training Centre, Abuja. He also completed training in Health, Safety and Environment (HSE Levels 1, 2 and 3), Environmental Impact Assessment, and Basic First Aid/CPR. In 2018, he completed training programmes in Governance and Health and Introduction to Early Childhood Development through the United States Agency for International Development (USAID). He also completed a three-month Planning Officer conversion training at the Public Service Institute of Nigeria, Abuja, in 2021.

PUBLIC SERVICE CAREER
Comrade Sunday has built his career in the Nigerian public service through several Federal Government institutions. He served as a Labour Officer II at the Federal Ministry of Labour and Employment, Asaba, Delta State, from September 2018 to November 2021. He subsequently served as a Planning Officer II at the Federal Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation, Abuja, from November 2021 to 2022. In October 2023, he joined the Office of the Head of the Civil Service of the Federation (OHCSF), where he served as a Planning Officer In June 2026, he moved to the Federal Ministry of Aviation and Aerospace Development, Abuja, where he currently serves as a Planning Officer in the Planning, Research and Statistics Department. His experience in public service has strengthened his knowledge of administration, planning, leadership and public-sector management.

ENTREPRENEURSHIP AND MEDIA CAREER
 Comrade Sunday’s passion for storytelling, filmmaking, videography and entertainment led him to establish Comrade Reality Show and Movie Series Limited, which he solely owns. Through this company, he pursued his interest in film production, entertainment and creative storytelling. Building on this experience, he founded SHOTOPOINT PRODUCTIONS alongside his colleague and strategic partner, Comrade Emmanuel Anointed Azagba, who serves as Co-Founder and Chief Operating Officer (COO). SHOTOPOINT PRODUCTIONS represents a broader vision for professional media production, creative innovation, technology and African storytelling. The company aims to serve audiences in Africa and around the world through quality productions and creative services. Its areas of focus include film and television production, documentaries, photography, commercials, digital media, post-production, creative development, talent development and casting, among other entertainment and media services.

YOUTH LEADERSHIP AND DEVELOPMENT

Beyond public service and media entrepreneurship, Comrade Sunday is the Founder and President of Young Leaders Frontier Movement, an initiative connected to his interest in leadership, youth development and creating opportunities for the next generation. His commitment to developing people is an important part of his personal and professional life. He believes that everyone has potential and deserves an opportunity to discover their abilities, develop their talents and succeed. This belief influences how he leads, builds teams and works with others. He values trust, teamwork, creativity and the contributions of people who share a common vision. Within SHOTOPOINT PRODUCTIONS, he believes in the collective abilities of his team. For him, success is not only about building an organisation but also about helping people grow, giving them opportunities and creating a platform where their ideas and talents can make a difference.

VISION FOR SHOTOPOINT PRODUCTIONS
 Comrade Sunday’s vision is to build SHOTOPOINT PRODUCTIONS into a modern African media company that combines creativity, technology and professional production to tell compelling stories and reach global audiences. He believes that African stories, talent and creativity deserve a stronger presence on the international stage. Through SHOTOPOINT PRODUCTIONS, he hopes to support emerging talents, create opportunities for aspiring filmmakers and entertainers, and contribute to the growth of the African creative industry. For him, SHOTOPOINT PRODUCTIONS is more than a business. It is an opportunity to bring people together, develop talent and build a creative platform with a lasting impact.

PERSONAL PHILOSOPHY
Comrade Sunday Arimokwu believes in taking action, creating opportunities and pursuing a vision with determination. His journey through public service, entrepreneurship, youth leadership and media production reflects his desire to make a meaningful contribution to society.

HIS PHILOSOPHY
“If you want to go global, don’t just start local, start now.” Through his work as a public servant, entrepreneur, youth leader and creative professional, Comrade Sunday continues to pursue his goal of developing people, supporting young talents and helping African media reach a wider global audience.
