Chief Executive Officer

At Shoto Point Productions, the Chief Executive Officer provides the strategic vision that connects creativity, people, partnerships and sustainable business growth. The role is centred on building a company that creates meaningful media while maintaining professional standards, strong relationships and a clear long-term direction.

Leadership & Vision

The CEO is responsible for defining the organisation's strategic direction, establishing priorities and creating an environment where creative and operational teams can perform at their best. This includes identifying opportunities, evaluating risks and ensuring that major decisions remain aligned with the company's mission and values.

Building Strategic Partnerships

A major part of the CEO's responsibility is developing trusted relationships with business partners, clients, media organisations and other stakeholders. Through open communication and solution-focused collaboration, the company aims to create partnerships that produce measurable value for everyone involved.

Creative & Business Growth

The CEO works across the creative and commercial sides of the organisation, supporting new ideas while ensuring that projects are properly planned, delivered and positioned for long-term growth. Innovation is encouraged when it solves a real problem or creates a better experience for the audience and partners.

People & Culture

Strong organisations are built by people. The CEO promotes professionalism, accountability, learning and respectful teamwork, with the goal of creating a workplace where individuals can contribute their expertise and grow with the company.
